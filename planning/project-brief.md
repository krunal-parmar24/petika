# Project Brief: Petika — Contemporary Single Page Portfolio

> **Template Name**: Petika  
> **Tagline**: The High-Impact Single Page Portfolio & Showcase for Creative Technologists  
> **Category**: Single Page Portfolio Website (with Dark & Light Theme)  
> **Distribution Standard**: UI8, ThemeForest (Envato), Creative Market  
> **Author**: Product Analyst & UI/UX Designer  
> **Date**: 2026-11-23  

---

## 1. Product Mission & Value Proposition

Petika is an elite, commercial-grade HTML5 + Bootstrap 5.3.x single-page portfolio template engineered specifically for senior product designers, creative technologists, full-stack design engineers, and freelance consultants who need a commanding digital presence that converts recruiters, founders, and enterprise clients in under 45 seconds.

Instead of fragmenting work across multiple confusing pages, Petika concentrates the professional narrative into an editorial Bento Grid experience. Visitors can effortlessly toggle between an immersive Obsidian & Radiant Amber Dark Mode and a pristine Alabaster Gallery Light Mode, review deep case study breakdowns via interactive in-page modals, filter projects smoothly, and initiate contact with zero friction.

### Core Value Pillars
1. **Neo-Editorial Bento Architecture**: Asymmetrical, modular grid cards creating visual rhythm, high scannability, and instant hierarchy for projects, skills, metrics, and biography.
2. **First-Class Dual Theme (Zero-FOUT)**: Dedicated, handcrafted visual experiences for both Dark (`[data-bs-theme="dark"]`) and Light (`[data-bs-theme="light"]`) modes with zero flash-of-unstyled-theme on reload.
3. **In-Page Case Study Modals**: Rich modal viewports displaying the full design story (Client Brief, Challenge, Solution, Tech Badges, Metrics, Live Demo) without breaking the single-page flow.
4. **Performance & Code Purity**: 100% Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript, native Bootstrap 5.3.x, zero jQuery, Core Web Vitals score >= 95, and 100% W3C HTML5 compliance.
5. **Instant Rebranding Ergonomics**: 3-tier CSS custom property token system allowing buyers to customize colors, typography, and borders in under 3 minutes.

## 2. Key Personas & Target Buyers

1. **Senior Product & UX/UI Designers**: Showcase design systems, Figma workflows, design strategy, and measurable product growth outcomes to hiring managers and enterprise tech clients.
2. **Creative Technologists & Design Engineers**: Bridge code and aesthetics by highlighting interactive prototypes, frontend architecture, and visual craft.
3. **Freelance Digital Consultants & Studio Founders**: Win high-ticket retainer contracts with social proof, client testimonials, transparent availability, and direct booking triggers.
4. **Full-Stack Developers**: Deliver an aesthetic, designer-grade portfolio that sets them apart from the sea of plain text developer resumes.

---

## 3. Brand Personality & Visual Tone

- **Aesthetic Tone**: Neo-Editorial Bento Elegance — bold, modern, confident, tactile, meticulously crafted.
- **Dark Mode Signature**: Deep obsidian canvas (`#0A0D14`), slate surface cards (`#111726`), warm radiant amber gold accents (`#F59E0B`), and cyber cyan metrics (`#06B6D4`).
- **Light Mode Signature**: Crisp alabaster canvas (`#F8FAFC`), pure white surface cards (`#FFFFFF`), warm obsidian typography (`#0F172A`), and refined amber bronze accents (`#D97706`).
- **Copywriting Tone**: Realistic, professional, metrics-driven, and confident without hyperbole ("Designed design system adopted by 120+ engineers", "Increased mobile conversion by +34%", "Available for Selected Q1/Q2 Engagements").
