## 1. Primary Commercial Buyers (Template Purchasers)

### Persona A: Elena Rostova — Senior Staff Product Designer
- **Role**: Staff Product Designer at an enterprise fintech scale-up; also undertakes high-ticket advisory consulting.
- **Pain Points**: Needs to showcase 6 flagship case studies with measurable business metrics, but hates maintaining a bloated WordPress site or paying $29/month for Webflow/Framer when she can host static HTML on Vercel/GitHub Pages for free.
- **Why Petika Wins Her**: The Bento layout organizes her accolades, design principles, and case study modals with high editorial taste. The dark/light theme switcher allows her to appeal to both Silicon Valley tech leads and traditional corporate banking clients.

### Persona B: Marcus Vance — Creative Technologist & Frontend Architect
- **Role**: Freelance Design Engineer bridging Figma design systems and React/TypeScript frontend implementation.
- **Pain Points**: Disgusted by portfolio templates packed with messy jQuery, broken layout shifts, and outdated Bootstrap 4 markup. Wants pristine, clean semantic HTML5 and CSS custom properties he can inspect with pride.
- **Why Petika Wins Him**: Zero jQuery, 100% Vanilla ES6+, CSS variable tokens, fast Lighthouse score (98+), and subtle micro-interactions that demonstrate technical sophistication.

### Persona C: Julian Chen — Digital Design Consultant & Indie Agency Lead
- **Role**: Founder of a boutique 2-person design studio offering brand identity, UI/UX, and web development.
- **Pain Points**: Needs a portfolio template that can be quickly rebranded with new client work and color palettes to pitch enterprise tenders.
- **Why Petika Wins Him**: Rebranding takes 60 seconds by altering `--color-accent` and `--font-display`. The client testimonials, experience timeline, and working validated contact form drive high inquiry conversions.

## 2. Secondary Audience (The Recruiter & Client Reviewer)

Recruiters, design directors, and startup founders visiting Petika evaluate candidates with specific behavioral habits:
- **Scan Window**: 30–45 seconds initial review.
- **Information Priorities**:
  1. *Who is this person and what is their specialty?* (Instant clarity in Hero Bento).
  2. *Are they currently available?* (Live pulsating availability status pill).
  3. *What have they actually built and what was the outcome?* (Filterable project cards + Quick-View modal with impact metrics).
  4. *Who vouches for them?* (Client testimonial quotes with verified LinkedIn/company credentials).
  5. *How do I contact them right now?* (Validated contact form + copyable email/phone pills).
