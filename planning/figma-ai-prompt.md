## 🎯 Master Prompt (<2,000 Characters — Copy & Paste directly into Figma AI)

```text
Design an elite single-page portfolio in Figma for "Petika", a Senior Product Designer & Creative Technologist. Create Dark & Light themes side-by-side (1440px desktop, 768px tablet, 375px mobile).

STYLE & MOOD:
Neo-Editorial Bento Elegance. Asymmetric bento grid, 24px radius, 1px subtle borders, soft elevation, glassmorphism.
- Dark Theme: Canvas #0A0D14, Cards #111726, Accent Amber #F59E0B, Cyan #06B6D4, Status Green #10B981, Text #F8FAFC/#94A3B8, Border rgba(255,255,255,0.08).
- Light Theme: Canvas #F8FAFC, Cards #FFFFFF, Accent Amber #D97706, Text #0F172A/#475569, Border #E2E8F0.
- Typography: Syne (Headings), Inter (Body), JetBrains Mono (Metrics/tags).

SECTIONS (Single Page Smooth-Scroll):
1. Sticky Glass Nav: "PETIKA" wordmark, "Available" green beacon, links (About, Work, Experience, Skills, Reviews, Contact), dark/light toggle pill, "Hire Me" CTA.
2. Bento Hero:
   - Intro (Col 8): Availability pill, H1 "Crafting High-Conversion Products & Scalable Systems", bio, "View Works" + "Get in Touch" buttons.
   - Portrait (Col 4): Photo card, "8+ Yrs FinTech/AI" badge, social links.
   - 3 Stat Badges: "$42M+ Capital", "120+ Engineers", "99.4% Rating".
3. About Bento: Bio narrative + 3 principles (Systems, Metrics, Speed) + UTC-8 status bar.
4. Filterable Portfolio: Category pills (All, Product, Web, Mobile, Brand) + 6 bento cards with metrics & "Quick View" modal trigger.
5. Case Study Modal: Prototype view, Challenge vs Solution, tech stack chips, impact stats, live link.
6. Career Timeline: 4 chronological role cards with company badges, dates, and metric bullets.
7. Skills Bento: 3 columns (Product Design, Frontend Code, Tools) with badges.
8. Testimonials: 3 cards with 5-star ratings, quotes, client avatars & titles.
9. Contact Hub: Left info card with copy pills (email, phone, location) + Right form (Name, Email, Budget, Message) + Send CTA.
10. Footer: Minimal copyright & back-to-top button.
```
